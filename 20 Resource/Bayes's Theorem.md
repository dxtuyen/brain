## Law of Total Probability
$$ \large
P(B) = \sum_{i=1}^k P(B|A_i) \cdot P(A_i) = P(B|A_1)P(A_1) + P(B|A_2)P(A_2) + \dots + P(B|A_k)P(A_k)
$$
## Bayes's Theorem 
$$ \large
P(A_j|B) = \frac{P(B|A_j) \cdot P(A_j)}{P(B)} = \frac{P(B|A_j) \cdot P(A_j)}{\sum_{i=1}^k P(B|A_i) \cdot P(A_i)}
$$
